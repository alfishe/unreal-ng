# Keyboard 3D models: the reference gallery

The photos POC 031 collected and measured, per keyboard, with what each is good for in 3D. The facts, colours and
measurements are in the POC 031 reference files: [sinclair.md](../../../tools/poc/031-authentic-osd-keyboards/references/sinclair.md),
[clones.md](../../../tools/poc/031-authentic-osd-keyboards/references/clones.md),
[next.md](../../../tools/poc/031-authentic-osd-keyboards/references/next.md),
[pc.md](../../../tools/poc/031-authentic-osd-keyboards/references/pc.md). The photos are references to measure and
compare against, never textures: no photo is traced or embedded in a model.

Use: **top** a straight-down view (footprint, positions, colours if the light is neutral); **3/4** a three-quarter
view (heights, profiles, roughly); **side** a level side view (heights, profiles); **detail** a close-up of a part;
**colour** neutral light, good for colours; **2D** the POC 031 picture the model must match from above.

`P` = `tools/poc/031-authentic-osd-keyboards/references/photos/`, `A` = `tools/poc/031-authentic-osd-keyboards/atlas/`.
