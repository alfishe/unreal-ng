## P-02B on the 48K and 128K

| Emulator | P-02B | Reading |
|:--|:--|:--|
| unreal-ng, FUSE, MAME (48K), xpeccy-plus | as expected | the byte at the end of the stretched read |
| ZXMAK2, Kozynax | the same bytes one tick later | their stock ULA is the "late" one: everything is one tick later |
| MAME (128K) | the same bytes two ticks later | as everything else on its 128K |
| ZEsarUX, SkoolKit | `#FF` throughout | the byte is not looked up at the end of the read |
| Xpeccy, ZX-M8XXX | other bytes | their floating bus differs (see their P-02) |

The three independent reads that agree (FUSE, MAME's 48K, ZXMAK2's pattern) back the probe's expected values,
which come from the probe's own model (research: docs/inprogress/2026-09-30-fusetest-core-defects/research.md).
