; --- tone polarity (row 2): we start high after power-on and RST, as SAASound
*                       mister    polarity  row 2: the RTL starts every tone generator low
*                       mame      polarity  row 2: MAME starts every tone generator low

; --- noise register (row 6): the RTL has a 17-bit register with other taps
*                       mister    noise     row 6: 17-bit LFSR, not the measured x^18 + x^11 + 1
mixer                   mister    out       row 6: noise-mixed voices follow the other LFSR
noise-from-tone         mister    out       row 6: noise-mixed voices follow the other LFSR
random-[0-9]            mister    out       row 6: noise-mixed voices follow the other LFSR
random-raw-*            mister    out       row 6: noise-mixed voices follow the other LFSR

; --- RST (row 9): Philips: numbers written during RST act only after the first half period
sync-reset              saasound  tone      row 9: SAASound applies numbers written during RST at once
sync-reset              mister    tone      row 9: the RTL reloads its counters from the live registers during RST
sync-reset              mister    out       row 9: follows the tone difference
sync-reset              mame      tone      row 9: MAME restarts the counters only at the #1C write
sync-reset              mame      noisetime row 9: MAME does not hold the generators during RST

; --- tone number alone (row 3)
octave-latch            saasound  tone      row 3: SAASound acts on a lone tone number one transition later

; --- noise rate switch (rows 7, 8)
mixer                   saasound  noisetime row 7: SAASound switches the noise rate at once (accumulator)
noise-from-tone         mister    noisetime row 8: leaving source 3 the RTL resumes its reload value (2048)
noise-from-tone         mame      noisetime row 8: MAME's divider keeps running in source 3

; --- envelope controls (rows 11, 13)
env-shapes-*            mister    env       row 11: the RTL keeps the enabling write pending, so a single shape plays twice
env-external            mister    env       row 11: the RTL keeps the enabling write pending, so a single shape plays twice
env-resolution-switch   mister    env       row 13: the RTL keeps the position LSB on a 3-to-4-bit switch

; --- MAME envelope model (rows 10-14): controls act at once and restart, 3-bit steps
; at half speed, both generators clock on either address, the first step is skipped
env-*                   mame      env       rows 10-14: MAME envelope model (immediate controls, 3-bit half speed)
all-voices              mame      env       rows 10-14: MAME envelope model (immediate controls, 3-bit half speed)
random-*                mame      env       rows 10-14: MAME envelope model (immediate controls, 3-bit half speed)

; --- MAME generators stop while #1C bit 0 is clear (row 16)
random-*                mame      tone      row 16: MAME stops every generator while the sound is disabled
random-*                mame      noisetime row 16: MAME stops every generator while the sound is disabled

; --- raw streams mix every behavior above; after the first such write the models
; legitimately run apart (they exist for the golden digests and smoke runs)
random-raw-*            saasound  *         raw stream: rows 3, 7, 9 occur
random-raw-*            mister    *         raw stream: rows 6, 8, 9, 11, 13 occur
